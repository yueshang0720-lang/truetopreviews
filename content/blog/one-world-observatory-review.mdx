From **1,250 feet above Manhattan**, you get a full 360° sweep of the skyline, the harbor, Brooklyn, New Jersey, and the entire arc of the city — up to **45 miles** in every direction.

But it's far more than a view. The visit is a choreographed journey: a 47-second cinematic elevator ride, a theater reveal, interactive exhibits, and optional sky-high dining. Here's everything you need to know before you go, from ticket prices to the best time to visit.

## Quick Facts at a Glance

- **Location:** One World Trade Center, 117 West Street, New York, NY 10007
- **Floors:** 100, 101, and 102
- **Height:** 1,250 feet above Manhattan
- **Views:** 360 degrees, up to 45 miles
- **Hours:** Daily 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM (seasonal)
- **Tickets:** from $39

## The Experience: Step by Step

### 1. Global Welcome Center and Horizon Grid

Your visit starts on the ground floor with the **Global Welcome Center**, a digital tapestry of visitors from around the world, and the **Horizon Grid**, an interactive exhibit that brings New York's history to life.

### 2. The SkyPod Elevator (47 Seconds)

The **SkyPod™ Elevator** takes you to the top in just **47 seconds**, playing a floor-to-ceiling time-lapse of New York's transformation from the 1500s to today. It's a clever piece of storytelling that makes the ride feel like a time machine.

### 3. See Forever Theater

Just before you step out, the **See Forever® Theater** delivers a short, cinematic reveal of the city — time-lapse imagery, music, and motion combined to create an emotional "wow" moment.

### 4. The Observatory: Floors 100, 101, and 102

Then the glass opens up and **New York unfolds in every direction at once** — Midtown, the harbor, Brooklyn, New Jersey, the Statue of Liberty, and the full skyline arc. There's no other vantage point in the city that gives you all of it simultaneously.

### 5. City Pulse and the Digital Skyline Guide

On the floor, **City Pulse™ Tour Ambassadors** — real New Yorkers — bring the landmarks and stories outside the glass to life. The **Digital Skyline Guide** (an interactive iPad experience) lets you tap any landmark to hear its story up close. It's included with Combination and All-Inclusive tickets.

## Sky-High Dining

You don't have to leave the sky to eat. **ONE Dine** on the 101st floor serves seasonally inspired plates with a full 360° panorama, while **ONE Mix** pours craft cocktails. Reservations are recommended. For something quicker, **illy Caffè & Gelateria** offers authentic Italian espresso, cold brew, and handcrafted gelato — with the same skyline views.

![ONE Dine restaurant](https://www.oneworldobservatory.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/OWO_Restaurant_Night_Corporate_1262_Full-scaled.jpg)

## Best Time to Visit and Pro Tips

- **Sunset is golden hour** — but also the busiest. Book an evening slot to catch the daytime skyline and the city lighting up after dark.
- **Go on a clear day.** Visibility is everything; check the forecast before committing to a time slot.
- **Midweek mornings are quietest** if you want room to breathe at the glass.
- **Give yourself 1.5–2 hours** to enjoy the exhibits, not just the view.

## How to Get There

One World Observatory is in the heart of Lower Manhattan, and **public transit is strongly recommended**:

- **Subway:** A/C to Chambers St, E to World Trade Center, 1 to WTC Cortlandt, 2/3 to Fulton St, R/W to Cortlandt, or 4/5 to Fulton St
- **Bus:** M20 or M55 to the WTC area
- **PATH (from New Jersey):** from Hoboken, Jersey City, or Newark
- **Ferry:** NY Waterway from several New Jersey terminals
- **Car:** parking at Brookfield Place (SP+ Parking) across West Street

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is One World Observatory worth it?

Yes — it's the highest and most complete observation experience in New York. If you only do one deck, the SkyPod elevator, See Forever Theater, and those 360° views make it a strong pick.

### How long does the visit take?

Plan 1.5 to 2 hours for the full experience, longer if you dine at ONE Dine.

### What's the difference between the ticket tiers?

Standard is views only. Combination adds priority entry and the Digital Skyline Guide. All-Inclusive adds flexible arrival and a bigger credit. VIP adds a 60-minute guided tour.

### Can I take photos?

Absolutely — photography for personal use is encouraged throughout the observatory.

### Is it wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the observatory is fully accessible.
